# Ev-Sales-Forcasting


 Electric Vehicle (EV) Adoption in India – Presentation
This project showcases a PowerPoint-style presentation that highlights the progress and trends in electric vehicle adoption in India. The deck is themed in a green visual palette, symbolizing sustainability and innovation in mobility.

📁 Chapters Covered
1️⃣ Introduction
Background: Understanding the evolution of the EV landscape and why it matters.

Global EV Outlook: Current global trends, market leaders, and the global push towards sustainable transportation.

EV Adoption Scenario in India: Insights into market growth, demand patterns, and rising awareness among Indian consumers.

Government Initiatives: Key policies like FAME, tax benefits, and state-level incentives.

Types of EVs in Indian Market: Overview of 2W, 3W, 4W, and commercial electric vehicles.

🧑‍🎓 Purpose
This presentation is designed for academic submissions, awareness programs, or EV policy research. It provides a balanced mix of visual storytelling and factual depth.

🌿 Theme
The entire deck follows a green energy theme to reflect the environmental impact of EVs and India’s transition toward clean mobility.
